I have applied AI in customer solutions since my time at Inrovo, where we licensed a speech model from A*STAR so users of our cloud robotics platform could control robots by voice. At EDB, I built an agentic marketing knowledge platform that finds the right source material and drafts content with citations, so the team can trust what it produces. From user needs to products

I start every product with the people who will use it. As a graduate at Barclays, I built a storage provisioning portal for both the teams requesting storage and the team providing it, saving 60 man-days a month. At Inrovo, I led our cloud robotics training platform as an in-house product. At EDB, discovery work with HR, finance, marketing and other teams led to an HR platform that complements Workday, now used by about 850 staff with 85% less time spent on HR tasks. Each engagement follows one of three engagement models, chosen by the size of the change and how ready the business is to lead it.

Delivering complex projects on schedule

Delivered S$18M+ across 11+ projects. Leading and growing AI-ready teams

At EDB, I lead 20 product managers and developers on a S$2M annual budget, built up from a small core team. I moved the team to an AI-first way of working, with AI supporting design, coding, testing, deployment, security testing and monitoring, and match how we engage each business team to one of three engagement models. This way of working built an HR platform used by about 850 staff and an office services super-app for about 800. My earlier teams prepared me for this and spanned five countries: 20 developers across the UK, Lithuania, Singapore and India at Barclays, and 6 developers in China at Inrovo, where being fluent in both Chinese and English helped bridge the two sides. My focus today is developing people so the team delivers independently.

Product engagements with customers

Three engagement models, matched to the request

Which model fits depends on two things: how big the change is, and how ready the business is to do its own design, framing and discovery. The model is agreed before work starts, so customers know what is expected of them.

  1. Model 1

    Collaborative

    Business is ready to do design, framing and discovery alongside us.

    BusinessHigh
    Product managerHigh
    DevelopersHigh
    • Business and product team discover the problem together, design the new process and sketch the screens side by side.
    • User stories are iterated, tested and released in sprints.
  2. Model 2

    Product manager-led

    Business isn't ready to lead that work yet, so the PM does it for them.

    BusinessLow
    Product managerHigh
    DevelopersHigh
    • Business hands over requirements. The PM digs out the context and real needs, designs the solution and briefs the developers.
    • Internal development sprints, with release sprints where the business tests the outcome.
  3. Model 3

    Developer-led

    Small change request: a label, an error message, a button.

    BusinessLow
    Product managerLow
    DevelopersHigh
    • Developers take the request straight from the user.
    • Skip discovery and solutioning; go straight to code and test.
    • The PM stays lightly involved for business matters such as cross-charging and other non-development work.
AI supports every model, end to end

  4. Testing
  5. CI/CD
  6. Security testing
  7. Monitoring
